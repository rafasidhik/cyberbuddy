Emergency Cybersecurity Guidance

Immediate actionable steps for high-risk situations.

Rule #1: STOP immediately! Never share OTPs, PINs, passwords, or CVVs. Legitimate banks and officials will NEVER ask for your verification code.
  • Do NOT send money or click links sent by the caller or messenger.
  • Hang up immediately if someone claims to be calling from your bank, courier, or police demanding urgent payment.
  • Verify independently: Call the company using the verified phone number printed on the back of your card or official website.
  • Block and report: Mark the number or sender as spam on WhatsApp/SMS.
Take rapid action before recovery details are changed:
  1. Use a clean, trusted device: Ensure your phone/PC isn't infected with malware.
  2. Change password immediately: If you can still log in, change it to a strong, unique 14+ character password.
  3. Change reused passwords: If you used that same password on email or other sites, update those instantly.
  4. Enable 2FA (Two-Factor Authentication): Prefer an Authenticator app (Google Authenticator, Aegis, Bitwarden).
  5. Log out all active sessions: Check Account Settings → Security → Active Sessions / Devices and sign out everything.
  6. Review recovery settings: Ensure your recovery phone number and backup email haven't been altered by the attacker.

1. Only Received the Link

Do NOT click it. Inspect the domain carefully. Block the sender and delete the message.

2. Clicked, But Entered Nothing

Close the browser tab immediately. Check your Downloads folder to ensure no automatic download occurred. Clear cache.

3. Entered Password / Credentials

Go to the real website on a trusted device and change your password right away. Turn on 2FA and terminate existing sessions.

4. Opened / Executed a File

Disconnect from Wi-Fi / Ethernet immediately to prevent malware from contacting command servers or spreading. Run a full antivirus scan.

Golden Hour Response (Within 2 Hours): Swift action maximizes chances of banks freezing transferred funds.
  • Call Your Bank Instantly: Dial the emergency fraud hotline on the back of your debit/credit card to freeze accounts or cards.
  • In India — National Cyber Fraud Helpline: Dial 1930 immediately to report financial fraud.
  • Official Reporting Portal: Register a formal complaint at cybercrime.gov.in.
  • Preserve Evidence: Take screenshots of transaction IDs, phone numbers, WhatsApp chats, and bank SMS messages. Do not delete them.
